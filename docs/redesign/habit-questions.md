# Expanded Habit Questions Framework

The current habit tracking system in HabitJoy uses basic categories (Exercise, Sleep, Nutrition, Social) that are too general to provide meaningful insights. This expanded framework transforms these broad categories into specific, measurable questions that provide actionable data and meaningful patterns.

## Exercise & Physical Activity

### Movement Tracking
- **Daily Steps**: "How many steps did you take today?"
  - Numeric input (0-100,000)
  - Benchmark ranges (< 5,000 sedentary, 5,000-7,499 low active, 7,500-9,999 somewhat active, 10,000+ active)
  - Option to sync with fitness trackers
  
- **Workout Completion**: "Did you complete a planned workout today?"
  - Yes/No
  - Option to specify workout type
  - Duration tracking

- **Activity Minutes**: "How many minutes of intentional physical activity did you do today?"
  - Numeric input (0-1440)
  - Categorized by intensity (light, moderate, vigorous)
  
- **Standing Breaks**: "Did you take standing/movement breaks during prolonged sitting?"
  - Frequency scale (Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Regularly, Consistently)
  - Option to use time-based intervals

### Exercise Types
- **Cardio Activity**: "Did you engage in cardiovascular exercise today?"
  - Yes/No with type selection (running, cycling, swimming, etc.)
  - Duration and intensity metrics
  
- **Strength Training**: "Did you perform strength/resistance exercises today?"
  - Yes/No with muscle groups targeted (legs, core, upper body, full body)
  - Sets/reps tracking option
  
- **Flexibility Work**: "Did you practice stretching or mobility exercises today?"
  - Yes/No with type (static stretching, dynamic stretching, yoga)
  - Duration tracking
  
- **Sports/Recreation**: "Did you participate in sports or recreational physical activities?"
  - Yes/No with activity type
  - Social vs. individual context

### Recovery & Body Awareness
- **Physical Energy**: "How would you rate your physical energy level today?"
  - Scale 1-10
  - Comparison to personal baseline
  
- **Muscle Soreness**: "Are you experiencing muscle soreness or recovery needs?"
  - Scale 1-10 with body map selection
  - Recovery activity tracking
  
- **Rest Day Intentionality**: "If taking a rest day, was this planned recovery?"
  - Planned/Unplanned
  - Reason tracking (recovery, schedule, motivation, injury, etc.)

### Activity Context
- **Indoor/Outdoor Activity**: "Did you spend time being active outdoors today?"
  - Yes/No with duration
  - Location type (urban, nature, water)
  
- **Active Transportation**: "Did you use active transportation today (walking/cycling for travel)?"
  - Yes/No with distance estimate
  - Purpose tracking (commute, errands, leisure)

## Sleep & Rest

### Sleep Quantity
- **Sleep Duration**: "How many hours of sleep did you get last night?"
  - Hour/minute input
  - Comparison to personal target
  - Option to sync with sleep tracker
  
- **Bedtime Consistency**: "Did you go to bed within 30 minutes of your target time?"
  - Yes/No with actual time
  - Deviation tracking
  
- **Wake Time Consistency**: "Did you wake up within 30 minutes of your target time?"
  - Yes/No with actual time
  - Alarm usage (yes/no/snoozed)

### Sleep Quality
- **Sleep Continuity**: "How would you rate your sleep continuity?"
  - Scale 1-10
  - Number of awakenings (if known)
  
- **Morning Feeling**: "How refreshed did you feel upon waking?"
  - Scale 1-10
  - Comparison to recent average
  
- **Deep Sleep Perception**: "Do you feel you got enough deep, restful sleep?"
  - Yes/No/Unsure
  - Dream recall (none, vague, vivid)

### Sleep Environment
- **Bedroom Darkness**: "Was your sleep environment appropriately dark?"
  - Yes/No with light source tracking
  - Light-blocking methods used
  
- **Bedroom Temperature**: "Was your bedroom temperature comfortable for sleep?"
  - Too cold/Comfortable/Too warm
  - Temperature setting if known
  
- **Bedroom Quietness**: "Was your sleep environment appropriately quiet?"
  - Yes/No with noise source tracking
  - Noise mitigation methods used
  
- **Electronic Exposure**: "Did you avoid screens 1+ hour before bedtime?"
  - Yes/No with device types if used
  - Blue light filtering usage

### Sleep Behaviors
- **Bedtime Routine**: "Did you follow your wind-down routine before sleep?"
  - Yes/Partially/No
  - Routine elements completed
  
- **Caffeine Cutoff**: "Did you avoid caffeine within 8 hours of bedtime?"
  - Yes/No with timing of last consumption
  - Amount consumed
  
- **Evening Meal Timing**: "Did you finish your last meal at least 3 hours before bedtime?"
  - Yes/No with timing of last meal
  - Meal heaviness (light/moderate/heavy)
  
- **Napping**: "Did you take any naps today?"
  - Yes/No with timing and duration
  - Refreshed feeling after (1-10)

## Nutrition & Hydration

### Meal Patterns
- **Meal Regularity**: "Did you eat regular meals today (avoiding long gaps or skipping)?"
  - Yes/Somewhat/No
  - Meal timing tracking
  
- **Mindful Eating**: "Did you eat mindfully, without distractions?"
  - All meals/Some meals/No meals
  - Distractions present if any
  
- **Eating Environment**: "Where did you consume your meals today?"
  - At table/Desk/On-the-go/In bed/Other
  - Social context (alone/with others)
  
- **Meal Planning**: "Were your meals planned in advance?"
  - All meals/Some meals/No meals
  - Preparation method (home-cooked, takeout, restaurant)

### Food Composition
- **Vegetable & Fruit Intake**: "How many servings of vegetables and fruits did you consume today?"
  - Numeric input (0-20+)
  - Variety tracking (number of different types)
  
- **Protein Sources**: "Did you include protein in your main meals today?"
  - All meals/Some meals/No meals
  - Protein types (animal, plant-based, mixed)
  
- **Whole Food Percentage**: "Approximately what percentage of your food today was whole/unprocessed?"
  - Percentage slider (0-100%)
  - Examples of foods in each category
  
- **Added Sugar**: "Did you limit added sugar consumption today?"
  - Yes/Somewhat/No
  - Conscious indulgence vs. unintended consumption

### Hydration
- **Water Intake**: "How much water did you drink today?"
  - Cups/ounces/liters input
  - Comparison to personal target
  
- **Hydration Timing**: "Did you drink water consistently throughout the day?"
  - Yes/Somewhat/No
  - Morning hydration status
  
- **Alternative Beverages**: "What other beverages did you consume today?"
  - Types (coffee, tea, soda, juice, alcohol)
  - Quantities of each
  
- **Hydration Signals**: "Did you experience any dehydration signals today?"
  - Yes/No with symptom selection (thirst, headache, dark urine, dry mouth)

### Nutrition Context
- **Hunger Awareness**: "Did you eat in response to physical hunger cues?"
  - Always/Mostly/Sometimes/Rarely
  - Non-hunger eating triggers if applicable
  
- **Fullness Respect**: "Did you stop eating when comfortably full?"
  - Always/Mostly/Sometimes/Rarely
  - Overfullness frequency and context
  
- **Food Satisfaction**: "Were your food choices satisfying and enjoyable?"
  - Scale 1-10
  - Satisfaction vs. nutrition balance
  
- **Special Circumstances**: "Were there special food circumstances today?"
  - Social event/Travel/Illness/Holiday/None
  - Adaptation strategies used

## Implementation in HabitJoy

### User Experience Considerations
- Questions appear contextually based on selected habit categories
- Progressive disclosure prevents overwhelming the user
- Quick input methods for regular tracking
- Deeper reflection options for periodic reviews
- Smart defaults based on user patterns
- Skip/not applicable options for all questions

### Data Analysis Potential
- Correlation detection between specific behaviors and mood/energy
- Trend visualization across habit categories
- Pattern identification for optimal routines
- Personalized insights based on individual responses
- Suggestion engine for habit improvements
- Progress tracking with meaningful metrics

### Onboarding Process
- Category interest selection during signup
- Gradual introduction of questions over time
- Personalized tracking plan development
- Example responses and guidance
- Habit stacking recommendations
- Custom question creation option

### Technical Implementation
- Question database with metadata (category, type, frequency)
- User preference storage for question selection
- Response history with timestamp and context
- API endpoints for question retrieval and answer submission
- Analytics processes for insight generation
- Notification system for relevant questions at appropriate times

### Customization Options
- Question frequency adjustment
- Category emphasis based on goals
- Question wording preferences
- Measurement unit selection
- Reminder timing for specific questions
- Custom question creation
- Question retirement for irrelevant items
