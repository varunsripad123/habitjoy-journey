HabitJoy

A full-stack habit tracking application with mood monitoring and payment integration

Application Overview

HabitJoy is a modern web application built with React, Node.js, Express, and MongoDB. It allows users to track their habits, monitor their mood, journal their thoughts, and visualize their progress over time.
